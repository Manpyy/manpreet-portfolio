Hey, I'm Manpreet 👋

I'm a Business Analyst with 3+ years of experience in Canada, currently based in London, Ontario. My work lives at the intersection of business strategy, data systems, and people — translating messy complexity into clear, actionable decisions.

I've had the privilege of working at TD Bank across multiple roles, growing from a Retail Banking Process Analyst into a full-fledged Banking Operations & Data Systems Analyst. Along the way, I've touched everything from payment lifecycle management to Power BI dashboards to AI-assisted workflows with Microsoft Copilot.

Before Canada, I co-founded Nebourhood, an Indian proptech startup in Bengaluru — which taught me more about product thinking, ambiguity, and resilience than any textbook ever could.

I hold a Postgraduate Diploma in Business and Information Systems Architecture from Fanshawe College and a Bachelor's in Information Technology from SRM University, Chennai.

When I'm not decoding requirements or grooming backlogs, you'll find me on the golf course, doom-scrolling reels (very Gen Z of me), or convincing myself that one more cup of coffee is a great idea. ☕
